## Overview

The Lost Citadel is an original campaign that takes players on a journey through ancient dwarven ruins, theological mysteries, and the slow revelation of a historical tragedy. This homebrew adventure offers a unique story while maintaining the quality and balance of official Pathfinder 2E content.

## What to Expect

- **Original Story**: Experience a completely unique narrative
- **Dwarven Heritage**: Deep dive into dwarven culture and history
- **Theological Elements**: Explore themes of faith and divine intervention
- **Mystery & Discovery**: Uncover clues and piece together ancient events
- **Custom Content**: Unique NPCs, locations, and encounters

## Perfect For

This campaign is ideal for players who enjoy:
- Original stories not found in published adventures
- Mysteries that unfold gradually over time
- Dwarven characters or interest in dwarven lore
- Mix of combat, exploration, and investigation

## Campaign Structure

The adventure unfolds across four interconnected arcs:
1. **Strange Lights** (Levels 3-5) - Investigation of the phenomenon
2. **The Sunken Halls** (Levels 6-8) - First exploration of the ruins
3. **Echoes of Faith** (Levels 9-10) - Theological revelations
4. **The Citadel's Secret** (Levels 11-12) - Final confrontation

## What Makes This Campaign Special

- Completely original story crafted specifically for Foundry VTT
- Custom maps, tokens, and atmospheric elements
- Flexible narrative that adapts to player choices
- Rich lore that rewards engaged players
- Professional quality comparable to published adventures
