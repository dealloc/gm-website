## Overview

Triumph of the Tusk offers a unique perspective on orc culture and diplomacy in the Hold of Belkzen. During the sacred Flood Truce, orc leader Ardax hosts diplomatic delegates from various nations and factions. As tensions rise and ancient grudges threaten to derail the peace talks, you'll navigate complex political intrigue, cultural conflicts, and threats both external and internal to ensure the success of this historic gathering.

## What to Expect

- **Orc Perspective**: Experience orc culture and society from within
- **Diplomatic Challenges**: Navigate complex political negotiations
- **Cultural Exploration**: Deep dive into Belkzen and orc traditions
- **Flood Truce**: Sacred time of peace tested by old conflicts
- **Political Intrigue**: Uncover plots and conspiracies
- **Mixed Encounters**: Combat, social challenges, and investigation

## Perfect For

This campaign is ideal for players who enjoy:
- Playing orc characters or exploring orc culture
- Diplomatic and social encounters alongside combat
- Political intrigue and complex negotiations
- Cultural immersion in non-traditional societies
- Mid-level play with meaningful choices
- Stories that challenge typical fantasy tropes

## Campaign Structure

The adventure unfolds across three interconnected books:

1. **Part 1** (Levels 3-6) - Arrival and initial diplomatic tensions
2. **Part 2** (Levels 7-10) - Deepening conflicts and hidden threats
3. **Part 3** (Levels 11-14) - Climactic resolution of the Flood Truce

## What Makes This Campaign Special

- Rare orc-focused narrative in Pathfinder
- Heavy emphasis on diplomacy and cultural understanding
- Explore the Hold of Belkzen in unprecedented detail
- Meaningful choices that affect regional politics
- Balance of combat, social encounters, and intrigue
- Challenge preconceptions about orc society
- Unique setting during the sacred Flood Truce
