## Overview

Outlaws of Alkenstar is a gritty urban adventure path set in the technological city-state of Alkenstar, where firearms and black powder reign supreme. Framed for crimes you didn't commit, you'll navigate the dangerous criminal underworld, greasy alleyways, and corrupt politics while seeking revenge against those who wronged you. This noir-inspired campaign combines western gunslinger themes with urban intrigue in one of Golarion's most unique locations.

## What to Expect

- **Gunslinger Focus**: Firearms and black powder weapons
- **Urban Adventure**: Navigate Alkenstar's dangerous streets
- **Revenge Story**: Hunt those who framed you
- **Criminal Underworld**: Deal with gangs and outlaws
- **Technology Theme**: Steampunk and industrial setting
- **Noir Atmosphere**: Gritty detective and crime drama
- **Moral Choices**: Navigate shades of gray in pursuit of justice

## Perfect For

This campaign is ideal for players who enjoy:
- Gunslinger and firearms-focused gameplay
- Urban adventures and city intrigue
- Revenge and noir storylines
- Technology and steampunk themes
- Criminal underworld exploration
- Moral ambiguity and tough choices
- Western-inspired fantasy

## Campaign Structure

The adventure unfolds across three gritty books:

1. **Part 1** (Levels 1-3) - Framed and seeking answers
2. **Part 2** (Levels 4-7) - Deep into the criminal underworld
3. **Part 3** (Levels 8-10) - Revenge and revelations
