## Overview

Kingmaker is the definitive kingdom-building adventure path, fully updated for Pathfinder Second Edition. Start as adventurers exploring the untamed Stolen Lands and grow into rulers of your own nation. This sandbox campaign combines classic adventuring with kingdom management, where your choices shape not just your story but an entire realm. From humble beginnings to legendary rulers, you'll face bandits, monsters, fey politics, and the challenges of governance itself.

## What to Expect

- **Kingdom Building**: Build and manage your own nation
- **Full 1-20 Campaign**: Complete progression from novice to legend
- **Sandbox Exploration**: Explore and chart the Stolen Lands
- **Political Intrigue**: Navigate rulership and diplomacy
- **Classic Adventure**: Updated version of beloved campaign
- **Management Systems**: Balance adventuring with governance
- **Legacy Building**: Create a lasting realm

## Campaign Structure

The adventure unfolds across six comprehensive books:

1. **Part 1** (Levels 1-3) - Exploring and claiming the Stolen Lands
2. **Part 2** (Levels 4-6) - Kingdom founding and early threats
3. **Part 3** (Levels 7-10) - Kingdom growth and fey complications
4. **Part 4** (Levels 11-14) - Political intrigue and external threats
5. **Part 5** (Levels 15-17) - Epic challenges to your realm
6. **Part 6** (Levels 18-20) - Final threats and legendary rulers
