## Overview

Gatewalkers is a mind-bending mystery adventure path that begins with a global amnesic episode—thousands of people,
including you, lost time and have no memory of what happened.
As paranormal investigators, you'll work to uncover the truth behind this Missing Moment,
following clues that lead you across multiple realities and planes of existence.
What starts as personal investigation evolves into a multiverse-threatening conspiracy.

## What to Expect

- **Mystery & Investigation**: Solve the puzzle of the Missing Moment
- **Amnesia Story**: Recover your lost memories
- **Planar Travel**: Journey across multiple realities
- **Paranormal Themes**: X-Files style investigation in Pathfinder
- **Reality Distortions**: Face threats from beyond your world
- **Personal Stakes**: Your own missing time drives the story
- **Multiverse Threats**: Uncover conspiracy spanning realities

## Perfect For

This campaign is ideal for players who enjoy:
- Mystery and investigation-focused gameplay
- Paranormal and X-Files style storytelling
- Planar travel and multiverse exploration
- Personal character-driven mysteries
- Starting at level 1 with engaging hooks
- Mind-bending reality-warping scenarios
- Uncovering conspiracies and hidden truths

## Campaign Structure

The adventure unfolds across three mind-bending books:

1. **Part 1** (Levels 1-3) - The Missing Moment and initial investigation
2. **Part 2** (Levels 4-6) - Planar travel and deepening mystery
3. **Part 3** (Levels 7-8) - Multiverse conspiracy revealed and resolved

## What Makes This Campaign Special

- Unique amnesia hook that personally involves all PCs
