## Overview

Abomination Vaults is the twenty-eighth Pathfinder Adventure Path and the first three-volume Pathfinder Adventure Path published by Paizo Inc.,
and ran from January 2021 to March 2021. It is loosely tied to the Second Edition Pathfinder Beginner Box,
which is also set in Otari, and provides an easier to manage first campaign for players and gamemasters.
It is also focused on a large dungeon complex, something Paizo had wanted to do for some time,
but Paizo considered that six volumes would be too much to keep interest in a single site.

## What to Expect

- **Megadungeon**: Multi-level complex to explore
- **Classic Dungeon Crawl**: Old-school exploration updated for 2E
- **Horror Atmosphere**: Dark experiments and abominations
- **Undead & Aberrations**: Face corrupted creatures
- **Vertical Progression**: Descend through multiple dungeon levels
- **Mystery Unraveling**: Discover Belcorra's dark history
- **Tactical Combat**: Challenging encounters in confined spaces

## Perfect For

This campaign is ideal for players who enjoy:
- Classic dungeon crawling adventures
- Horror themes and dark atmosphere
- Exploration and discovery
- Tactical combat in dungeon environments
- Uncovering mysteries layer by layer
- Traditional adventuring with modern rules
- Iconic megadungeon experience

## Campaign Structure

The adventure descends through three massive books:

1. **Ruins of Gauntlight** (Levels 1-3) - Upper levels and lighthouse
2. **Hands of the Devil** (Levels 4-7) - Middle depths and horrors
3. **Eyes of Empty Death** (Levels 8-10) - Deepest secrets revealed

## What Makes This Campaign Special

- Modern megadungeon design with 2E mechanics
- Three full books of dungeon exploration
- Rich history of Belcorra Haruvex to uncover
- Perfect balance of exploration, combat, and mystery
- Iconic Gauntlight lighthouse as central landmark
- Horror atmosphere without overwhelming darkness
- One of PF2E's most popular adventure paths
- Excellent introduction to dungeon crawling
