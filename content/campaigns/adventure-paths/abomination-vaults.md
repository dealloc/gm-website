## Overview

Abomination Vaults is a classic megadungeon adventure path taking you deep beneath the ominous Gauntlight lighthouse. Explore the multi-level dungeon complex where the mad wizard Belcorra Haruvex conducted horrific experiments. Each level reveals new horrors, dark secrets, and terrible abominations as you descend deeper into one of Pathfinder 2E's most iconic dungeon crawls. Perfect for fans of traditional dungeon exploration with modern design.

## What to Expect

- **Megadungeon**: Multi-level complex to explore
- **Classic Dungeon Crawl**: Old-school exploration updated for 2E
- **Horror Atmosphere**: Dark experiments and abominations
- **Undead & Aberrations**: Face corrupted creatures
- **Vertical Progression**: Descend through multiple dungeon levels
- **Mystery Unraveling**: Discover Belcorra's dark history
- **Tactical Combat**: Challenging encounters in confined spaces

## Perfect For

This campaign is ideal for players who enjoy:
- Classic dungeon crawling adventures
- Horror themes and dark atmosphere
- Exploration and discovery
- Tactical combat in dungeon environments
- Uncovering mysteries layer by layer
- Traditional adventuring with modern rules
- Iconic megadungeon experience

## Campaign Structure

The adventure descends through three massive books:

1. **Ruins of Gauntlight** (Levels 1-3) - Upper levels and lighthouse
2. **Hands of the Devil** (Levels 4-7) - Middle depths and horrors
3. **Eyes of Empty Death** (Levels 8-10) - Deepest secrets revealed
