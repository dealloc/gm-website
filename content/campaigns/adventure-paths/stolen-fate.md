## Overview

Stolen Fate is an epic high-level adventure path centered around the legendary Deck of Destiny—a mysterious artifact whose scattered cards hold reality-altering power. As high-level heroes, you'll race against time and rival factions to gather these powerful cards before they fall into the wrong hands. This globe-spanning quest will test your abilities to their limits as you face cosmic threats and make choices that could reshape the very fabric of reality.

## What to Expect

- **High-Level Epic Play**: Start at level 11, reach mythic level 20
- **Artifact Hunt**: Race to collect the scattered Deck of Destiny cards
- **Reality-Altering Stakes**: Control fate itself
- **Global Adventure**: Travel across Golarion and beyond
- **Rival Factions**: Compete with others seeking the cards
- **Harrow Themes**: Deep connection to Varisian fortune-telling magic
- **World-Changing Consequences**: Your choices reshape reality

## Perfect For

This campaign is ideal for players who enjoy:
- High-level play with powerful abilities
- Artifact-hunting and collection quests
- Reality-bending magic and cosmic threats
- Globe-spanning adventures
- Racing against rival groups
- Epic stakes affecting all of Golarion

## Campaign Structure

The adventure unfolds across three epic books:

1. **Part 1** (Levels 11-13) - Discovery and the hunt begins
2. **Part 2** (Levels 14-17) - Global search and rival confrontations
3. **Part 3** (Levels 18-20) - Final cards and reality-altering climax
