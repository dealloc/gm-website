## Overview

Shades of Blood is a vampire-themed adventure path set in the mysterious remnants of ancient Azlant. When a column of shadow erupts from the sea and washes across the sky, dimming the sun and unleashing shadowy monsters, you're recruited as research assistants to investigate this supernatural phenomenon. What begins as academic research quickly becomes a desperate race to stop an ancient vampire from using Azlanti magical technology to permanently shroud the world in darkness.

## What to Expect

- **Vampire Horror**: Face the undead and their dark machinations
- **Ancient Azlant**: Explore ruined Azlanti prisons and magical technology
- **Investigation & Discovery**: Uncover the truth behind the shadow column
- **Rising Stakes**: From research assistants to saviors of the world
- **Shadowy Threats**: Battle creatures born from unnatural darkness
- **Race Against Time**: Stop the vampires before the sun is lost forever

## Perfect For

This campaign is ideal for players who enjoy:
- Horror themes and vampire stories
- Investigation and mystery-solving
- Exploring ancient civilizations and lost technology
- Starting at level 1 and building to epic confrontations
- Dark atmosphere with high stakes
- Mix of combat, exploration, and problem-solving

## Campaign Structure

The adventure unfolds across three interconnected books:

1. **Thirst for Blood** (Levels 1-3) - Initial investigation and discovery
2. **The Broken Palace** (Levels 4-7) - Deep into the vampire conspiracy
3. **Part 3** (Levels 8-10) - Final confrontation to save the sun
