## Overview

Spore War is an epic high-level adventure path that pits you against one of Golarion's most dangerous threats: the demon lord Treerazer. From the corrupted swamp of Tanglebriar, this ancient demon threatens the very existence of the elven nation of Kyonin with fungal plagues and demonic armies. As high-level heroes, you'll venture into one of the most dangerous regions in the Inner Sea to stop a threat that could consume an entire nation.

## What to Expect

- **High-Level Epic Adventure**: Start at level 11, reach level 20
- **Demon Lord Threat**: Face Treerazer and his demonic legions
- **Fungal Horror**: Battle corrupted nature and spore-infested terrors
- **Elven Nation at Stake**: Save Kyonin from total annihilation
- **Tanglebriar Swamp**: Explore one of Golarion's most dangerous regions
- **Epic Battles**: Combat encounters worthy of legendary heroes

## Perfect For

This campaign is ideal for players who enjoy:
- High-level play with powerful abilities
- Epic stakes and world-threatening dangers
- Demon-hunting and battling corrupted nature
- Elven lore and Kyonin's history
- Challenging tactical combat
- Being legendary heroes who save nations

## Campaign Structure

The adventure unfolds across three epic books:

1. **Part 1** (Levels 11-13) - Initial conflict with Treerazer's forces
2. **Part 2** (Levels 14-17) - Deep into Tanglebriar's corruption
3. **Part 3** (Levels 18-20) - Final confrontation with Treerazer
