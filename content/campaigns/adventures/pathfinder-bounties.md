## Overview

Pathfinder Bounties are a collection of short, focused adventures that can be played as standalone one-shots or woven into ongoing campaigns. Each bounty presents a self-contained mission with clear objectives, making them perfect for single-session play or as side quests. With adventures across various levels and themes, Bounties offer flexibility for groups looking for quick, satisfying adventures that fit any schedule.

## What to Expect

- **Short Adventures**: Complete stories in 2-3 hours
- **Variable Levels**: Multiple bounties at different levels
- **Focused Missions**: Clear objectives and goals
- **Standalone Play**: No campaign knowledge required
- **Flexible Use**: One-shots or campaign supplements
- **Variety**: Different themes and challenges
- **Quick Setup**: Easy to prep and run
