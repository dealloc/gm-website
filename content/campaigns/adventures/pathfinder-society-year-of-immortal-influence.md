## Overview

Pathfinder Society: Year of Immortal Influence brings the organized play experience to your table with interconnected scenarios set during a tumultuous year for the Pathfinder Society. What begins as a charity event investigation in Absalom evolves into a potential murder mystery with far-reaching implications. With 13 scenarios spanning multiple levels, this offers flexible adventure options for groups of all experience levels.

- **Multiple Scenarios**: 13 different adventures to choose from

## Year of Immortal Influence

The Pathfinder Society's involvement in an Absalom charity event takes a dark turn when signs point to potential murder. Across 13 scenarios spanning multiple levels, agents investigate this mystery while dealing with various threats and challenges throughout the City at the Center of the World.

**Scenario Count**: 13 adventures
**Level Range**: 1-12
**Setting**: Primarily Absalom
**Theme**: Murder mystery and charity investigation
