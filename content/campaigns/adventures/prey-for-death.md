## Overview

Prey for Death is a thrilling high-level standalone adventure featuring the infamous Red Mantis assassins. When members of this legendary assassin organization are framed for crimes they didn't commit, they must use all their deadly skills not to kill, but to uncover the truth. This level 14 adventure offers experienced players a chance to explore the shadowy world of Golarion's most feared assassins while navigating betrayal, intrigue, and shocking revelations.

## What to Expect

- **High-Level Play**: Level 14 characters with powerful abilities
- **Red Mantis**: Play as or alongside legendary assassins
- **Intrigue & Betrayal**: Uncover conspiracy and treachery
- **Clear Your Names**: Prove innocence amid accusations
- **Shocking Revelations**: Discover hidden truths
- **Assassination Themes**: Stealth, precision, and deadly skills
- **Standalone Adventure**: Complete story in one adventure
