## Overview

Crown of the Kobold King is a beloved classic adventure updated for Pathfinder 2E. The frontier town of Falcon's Hollow faces multiple threats: undead rising from the nearby cemetery, aggressive kobold tribes, and a necromancer with sinister plans. This standalone adventure offers a perfect blend of classic fantasy adventuring with investigation, exploration, and combat across a compact but satisfying story.

## What to Expect

- **Classic Adventure**: Updated PF1E favorite for 2E
- **Multiple Threats**: Face undead, kobolds, and necromancers
- **Town Defense**: Protect Falcon's Hollow
- **Varied Encounters**: Combat, investigation, and exploration
- **Complete Story**: Satisfying beginning, middle, and end
- **Levels 1-6**: Good progression range
- **Standalone**: No prior campaign knowledge needed
