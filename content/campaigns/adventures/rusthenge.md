## Overview

Rusthenge is a compact standalone adventure set against the backdrop of New Thassilon's emergence. Ancient Thassilonian ruins have been discovered, and within them lie secrets both wondrous and dangerous. This level 1 adventure serves as a perfect introduction to Pathfinder 2E, featuring exploration of ancient magic, mysterious ruins, and the fascinating blend of ancient and modern that defines New Thassilon.

## What to Expect

- **Level 1 Adventure**: Perfect for new characters
- **Thassilonian Ruins**: Explore ancient magical sites
- **New Thassilon**: Experience this unique modern/ancient blend
- **Ancient Magic**: Discover forgotten secrets
- **Compact Story**: Complete adventure in one session
- **Beginner-Friendly**: Great introduction to PF2E
- **Mystery & Exploration**: Focus on discovery
