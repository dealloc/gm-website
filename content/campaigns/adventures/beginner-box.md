## Overview

ake the first step into an amazing world of fantasy adventure!
The Pathfinder Beginner Box contains everything you need to learn how to play the Pathfinder Roleplaying Game,
including rules to create your own fantasy hero and tools to make your own amazing stories.

Will you be a courageous fighter, charging headfirst into battle, or a sly rogue, moving quietly to strike at foes from the shadows?
Maybe you will be a knowledgeable wizard, wielding incredible arcane spells or a wise and pious cleric,
using the power of your deity to shape the world for the better. It is all up to you!

## What to Expect

- **Beginner-Friendly**: Simplified rules for easy learning
- **Tutorial Design**: Gradually introduces mechanics
- **Pre-Generated Characters**: Jump right into playing
- **Complete Adventure**: Levels 1-4 with full story
- **Teaching Tools**: Resources for new GMs
- **Foundry VTT Ready**: Digital version available
- **Learn by Playing**: Mechanics introduced through gameplay

## Perfect For

This campaign is ideal for players who:
- Are new to Pathfinder 2E
- Want to learn TTRPG basics
- Need simplified introduction to PF2E
- Want to teach others the game
- Prefer gradual learning curve
- Are new to GMing
- Want accessible entry to Pathfinder

## What's Included

- **Simplified Rulebook**: Core mechanics made accessible
- **Pre-Generated Characters**: Ready-to-play heroes
- **Introductory Adventure**: Menace Under Otari (Levels 1-4)
- **Character Creation Guide**: Learn to build characters
- **GM Resources**: Tools for running your first game
- **Foundry VTT Module**: Digital play support
- **Progression Path**: Clear advancement from 1-4
